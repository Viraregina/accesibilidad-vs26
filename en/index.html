03 · BEFORE AND DURING YOUR VISIT

Plan your schedule.
Organise your participation.

01Entrances and ramps ＋

Review each venue profile to identify entrances and ramps. In buildings with several rooms, the entrance you need may depend on your activity. View venue information ↓

02Seating and location within rooms ＋

If you need a specific location or seating arrangement, let the Hospitality team know in advance so they can plan for your arrival and, where relevant, your support person.

03Accreditation and support persons ＋

Review the conditions of your accreditation type. If you are attending with a support person, contact the Hospitality team before you arrive to coordinate their access.

04Wayfinding and breaks ＋

Ask venue staff for directions to your room or entrance. You can also ask about nearby pick-up and drop-off points.

05Restrooms and services ＋

The venue profiles include the available information about restrooms and other services. View venues ↓
