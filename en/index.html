03 · BEFORE AND DURING YOUR VISIT

Coordinate
your participation.

If you need any of the following support, reply to this email before the event. Tell us what you need and which activities you plan to attend, so the team can review your schedule and coordinate in advance with the relevant room staff. You do not need to share a diagnosis or medical information: simply describe the specific support you need.

01Arrival and venue access ＋

If you have difficulty getting from your arrival point to the entrance, mention it in your reply so the team can plan assistance and direct you to the appropriate access point. View venue information ↓

02Orientation inside the venues ＋

Room staff can direct you to your activity, lifts, accessible restrooms and other services, or accompany you along the route when this has been arranged in advance.

03Support person or guide dog ＋

If you need to attend with a support person or guide dog in order to participate, mention this in your reply. The team will review the necessary information and coordinate the relevant access arrangements.

04Waiting and seating ＋

If you need a seat while waiting or need to avoid standing for extended periods, let the team know in advance and remind room staff when you arrive.

05Location within the room ＋

If you need a particular location in order to follow an activity, mention it in your reply. Placement will be coordinated according to the room, the activity and availability.
